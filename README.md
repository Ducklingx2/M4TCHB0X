# M4TCHB0X

> **Seven Matches. One Spark. Trust No Match.**

M4TCHB0X is a browser-based multiplayer social deduction game inspired by the
*M4TCH80X* concept.

Seven players enter the Matchbox.

Everyone looks the same.

Everyone has a different identity.

And somewhere among them is the **Spark**.

The Spark wants to eliminate the other Matches without being discovered.
The **Snuffer** is trying to stop them.

Then things get complicated.

---

## 🔥 The Game

A match consists of **7 players**.

Every player receives:

- A random identity
- An identical appearance
- A bow and arrow
- Access to signs for communication during the round

There are two permanent special roles:

### Spark

The Spark secretly marks players.

At the end of the round, a marked player dies unless protected by the Snuffer.

The Spark can also swap locations with another player.

### Snuffer

The Snuffer secretly protects one player by marking them.

If the Snuffer marks the same player targeted by the Spark, that player survives.

### Flicker

Flicker is a temporary event, not a permanent role.

At a random point during a round, one player temporarily disappears from everyone
else's perspective.

They are still physically present.

Nobody is told who the Flicker is.

The Flicker can use the confusion however they want, including assisting the
Spark.
